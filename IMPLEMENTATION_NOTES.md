## Shortlist policy
This is a discussion shortlist, not a final weighted allocation. Foundations, minors and incomplete capacity cases receive guidance instead of securities assignments. Near-term routes show short-maturity debt discussion categories; longer-horizon choices use category screening. Within a category, supplied rating descending and source row ascending select an idea. Lump-sum ideas must individually pass the supplied minimum and restriction screen; the list is alternatives, not an instruction to buy all. Monthly-only cases need verified SIP minimums and availability. Stock candidates use the existing conservative long-horizon/experience/capacity gate and display the first four distinct sectors in source order. Full source catalogue is searchable and separate from the shortlist. No prices, targets, return promises, final weights or share quantities are invented.

The sheets do not include company research theses. Rationale is clearly identified as explanatory portfolio-role/category reasoning, not an attributed Geojit thesis. Full suitability, product terms, concentration and current research validity require review before investing.

## Journey and protection
11 individual prompts across four chapters; choice buttons advance automatically after 340 ms. Amount controls remain editable with Continue for lump sum plus optional SIP. Back cancels queued advancement, edit returns to review, reduced-motion users receive instant transitions. Floating journey and result navigation support mobile and desktop. Insurance cards explain importance, personal relevance, reflection questions and next steps; insurance is not assigned an investment percentage.
